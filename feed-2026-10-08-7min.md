**3. EU regulator orders phase-out of non-compliant stablecoins, including USDT**

European regulators are moving against the world's largest stablecoin, a cryptocurrency designed to hold a steady value of one U.S. dollar. The European Securities and Markets Authority issued guidance today telling licensed crypto firms to stop offering services for stablecoins that do not meet EU rules, including Tether's USDT, by January eighth, twenty twenty-seven. New purchases are to stop immediately, with existing holdings wound down under supervision.
